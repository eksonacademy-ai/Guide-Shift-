The opportunity

A fixed deadline creates demand that does not wait for budget cycles

After 31 December 2026 these products receive no security updates, fixes or technical support. Customers cannot postpone the decision.

01

A hard deadline

Microsoft stopped new purchases and renewals in November 2025. Support ends this December.

02

No direct successor

Microsoft names no like-for-like replacement, so customers must choose a new platform.

03

Hardware at end of line

HoloLens 2 production ended in late 2024, which pushes customers toward multi-device platforms.

04

Content at risk

Microsoft's migration tool only copies guides between its own Dataverse environments, so years of procedures need a new home.

Why this work matters

Guided work is part of how these plants run

  • Operations cannot pause. Assembly, maintenance and safety procedures run every shift.
  • It speeds up training. Sanofi reported cutting new-operator training from eight weeks to six with Guides and HoloLens 2.
  • It protects expertise. Procedures capture know-how that retiring staff take with them.
  • It is a long-term relationship. A migration leads to rollout, 3D content, training and support.
